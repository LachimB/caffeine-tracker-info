CAFFEINE TRACKER

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Your journal starts on your phone. Connecting Google Drive is a choice you make, and the app uses that connection only for the export, backup and recovery features described here.

Feature availability

The day review update adds explicit complete and caffeine-free confirmations in Today and History. Entry changes reopen affected dates, and the latest review action can be undone. An optional evening reminder is independent of caffeine targets. Existing journal entries, recipes and caffeine averages keep their meaning.

1. Who provides the app

Caffeine Tracker is an independent personal Android caffeine journal developed and maintained by LachimB (the developer). This policy covers the Android app and this information website. General support and privacy questions can be directed to the public support tracker; see the contact guidance below before posting.

At a glance
  • Local journaling does not require a Google account.
  • Optional Drive transfers go directly between the app and Google services, not through a developer-operated journal server.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics SDK or automated crash-reporting service.
  • The developer does not receive your journal or Google account data through normal app use.

2. Data the app accesses and stores

The app stores the drink details you enter: drink type and name, caffeine amount, serving volume when relevant, selected date and time, time zone, creation timestamp and optional notes. It also stores your settings, unfinished drink and correction drafts, history selections and operational information needed to display statistics, notifications, export status and queued work. Explicit day reviews store the recorded date, completeness state, stable record identity, revisions, review timestamps, export association and temporary Undo snapshots. Optional evening reminder settings and previously notified dates are stored locally to schedule reminders and prevent duplicates; they are included in full app backups.

The editable drink catalogue is stored locally with caffeine definitions, optional espresso equivalents and caffeine per espresso, reference and default serving volumes, named serving options and their caffeine overrides, display order, archived status, and any existing coffee or tea parent relationship. A recorded drink choice keeps the selected drink and any existing parent names/identities, usual or alternative role, source and consumed drink families, and selected serving details. These historical details let choice summaries remain accurate after the catalogue changes.

Corrections also store entry revisions, original undo deadlines, request receipts that prevent repeated saves, and pending/finalized daily-alert dates. Earlier entry values may be retained for an active undo window or pending export reconciliation.

If you connect Google Drive, the app uses your selected Google account's email address and Drive account identifier to associate the correct account with your destination. It stores authorized folder and file identifiers, export associations, authorization receipts and transfer status on the phone. These details prevent accidental exports to the wrong destination and let the app retry its work.

Google handles account selection and authorization. The app obtains access tokens through Google's Android authorization services to make authorized API requests. It does not ask for or receive your Google password. Tokens and device authorization are not included in the app's full backups.

The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, Gmail or unrestricted Drive access. Android notification permission is optional and is used for app alerts, the independently optional evening review reminder and transfer results. Review reminders use the device time zone and do not require a caffeine target, location access or exact-alarm permission.

3. How optional Google Drive access works

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits work with files the app creates and existing files or folders you explicitly authorize through Google. Selecting a parent folder does not automatically grant access to all existing contents. The app does not request separate email or profile scopes; account identity is obtained through the selected account and Drive's account information.

Within that authorization, the app can:

  • Create and update individual drink JSON exports, including date, caffeine amount, timestamps, optional volume and notes. Recorded drink-choice exports also identify the explicit relationship and serving snapshot, including 0 mg alternatives; use a reader that understands their labelled record version. Corrections update the linked file using its original identity; changing the recorded date moves that file into the appropriate dated folder. For gaps between recorded dates, it may add markers explicitly labelled as unconfirmed intake. An empty date does not establish a caffeine-free day. Recognized older gap files keep their inferred provenance and do not establish explicit confirmation. Explicit day reviews use separate, versioned day-status JSON records with their date, completeness, record identity, revision and review timestamps; reopening a day exports an unconfirmed review state. These records are distinct from consumed drinks and recorded choices. External changes or inaccessible saved files stop reconciliation without discarding the local review or silently replacing remote identities.
  • Create manual JSON history snapshots for all records or a chosen date range, including explicit day-review records for dates without drinks, and replace an existing snapshot when you confirm.
  • Create full app backups containing the journal database, settings, saved account and Drive associations, operational records, drink recipes, serving-size definitions and recorded choice snapshots, pending correction/undo state and unfinished drafts; read authorized backup files when you choose recovery.
  • Read file and folder metadata to verify access and organize associated exports into drinks/, exports/ and backups/.
  • Delete a linked individual drink export when you delete its journal entry, and move eligible older app backups to Drive Trash according to your retention setting.

Drive is used for these visible app features only. Changes made to exported drink files in Drive are not imported into your journal. Restoring a full backup is a separate action that replaces local app state after your confirmation; it is not continuous synchronization between devices.

You can choose Wi-Fi or mobile data, Wi-Fi only, or disable Drive transfers in Settings. Android scheduling, connectivity and Google authorization can delay work. Automatic drink changes and automatic full backups wait for active journal undo windows. Explicitly requested snapshots capture the chosen historical state, including original pending deadlines. An already submitted request may finish after a setting or entry changes.

Optional AI caffeine estimates

AI estimates are disabled until you add your own OpenAI API key in Settings. Tapping the wand explicitly sends the drink name you entered and an optional reference or serving volume directly to OpenAI, with an instruction to estimate caffeine and explain serving assumptions. The app does not include journal history, notes, Google account information or Drive identifiers in that request. Drink names are user-entered text: avoid personal information in names you send.

The API key is sent only to OpenAI for authentication. It is encrypted using Android Keystore and stored privately on the device, separately from the journal database. It is not included in drink exports, portable full backups or the local journal recovery copy. Remove the key in Settings to disable new estimates; clearing app storage also removes the stored key. Restoring a journal backup does not configure or replace a device key.

Requests are sent only when you ask, without automatic retries. Suggestions are approximate, may be wrong, and are shown for review before you choose to use them. The suggested value is then an editable recipe value. The app requests that OpenAI not store the response as a saved API response; this does not remove any provider-side operational or abuse-monitoring retention. OpenAI processes requests under its applicable policies and your API account settings. See OpenAI API data controls. API usage charges may apply to your account. Removing a key cannot cancel a request already sent.

4. Sharing and use of Google user data

The app sends journal exports, backup contents and the account/file information necessary to perform your requested operations to Google services. Google processes that information under its own privacy policy and applicable service terms. Files remain in your Google Drive account and are subject to the sharing permissions you set there. Anyone you grant access to a destination or file may be able to read it.

The developer does not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models. The app does not send this data to the developer for human review. If you voluntarily provide information in a support request, that information is used to respond to the request; do not send journal data or credentials.

Caffeine Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention, disconnection and deletion

On the phone: archiving a drink removes it from the logging list while retaining its definition. Removing a drink also removes its linked alternative definitions from the catalogue, without deleting journal entries or their recorded choice details. Past choices keep their recorded details after drink recipes are renamed, edited or archived. Journal records remain until you delete them or remove the app's local storage. Deleting a record hides it from History and, if it has a linked Drive export, queues removal of that export. Deletion is undoable during the configured window. Pending deletion records and associations can remain locally until authorized work finishes. Reopening a reviewed day records it as unconfirmed and retains its review and export identity; it does not erase historical snapshots or backups. Drink and day-review Undo before-snapshots are cleared when undone or expired, and older export-attempt payloads are removed after reconciliation. Successful deletion clears retained entry payloads; minimal action receipts can remain to prevent delayed callbacks recreating deleted data. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app's private local data, including its local recovery copy, and does not delete existing Drive files.

On Google Drive: individual exports and manual snapshots remain until removed by the app's described actions or by you in Drive. Deleting a journal entry queues permanent deletion of its linked individual export when authorized transfers are available. It does not remove that entry from previously created manual snapshots or backups. Manage those copies separately in Drive.

Full backups: the default is one backup per day with five compatible copies retained after Drive is connected. You can change the schedule and copy count or turn automatic backups off. Older eligible backups are moved to Drive Trash only after a new backup has been uploaded and verified. Trash contents follow Google's retention rules. A local safety copy of the state before the last restore is kept for undo and replaced at the next restore; removing app storage also removes it.

Disconnecting: Settings → Disconnect Google Drive pauses the app's saved connections. It preserves your local journal, saved associations and existing Drive files; it is not deletion or Google permission revocation. You can separately revoke access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase files already created. Remove unwanted exports, snapshots, backups and any remaining Trash copies in Drive yourself.

You can inspect, export and delete your own data using the app and Drive. The developer has no central copy of your journal to retrieve or delete. For privacy requests about information you voluntarily shared through support, use the contact route below without publicly posting the information itself. Applicable privacy rights are not limited by this policy.

6. Security and backup sensitivity

Local data is stored in the app's private Android storage. Drive requests use HTTPS and limited file authorization. The app disables Android's standard app backup mechanism and provides its own optional Drive backup feature.

Exports and backups are readable JSON files; a full backup contains an encoded database. Encoding and checksums are not encryption. The app does not add end-to-end encryption to these files. Use a private Drive folder, protect your device and Google account, and avoid sensitive details in journal notes. No storage or network service can guarantee absolute security.

Please do not post personal records, email addresses, account IDs, screenshots containing personal data, backup files, access tokens or credentials.
