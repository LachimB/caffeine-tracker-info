CAFFEINE TRACKER

Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Your journal starts on your phone. Connecting Google Drive is a choice you make, and the app uses that connection only for the export, backup and recovery features described here.

1. Who provides the app

Caffeine Tracker is an independent personal Android caffeine journal developed and maintained by LachimB (the developer). This policy covers the Android app and this information website. General support and privacy questions can be directed to the public support tracker; see the contact guidance below before posting.

At a glance
  • Local journaling does not require a Google account.
  • Optional Drive transfers go directly between the app and Google services, not through a developer-operated journal server.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics SDK or automated crash-reporting service.
  • The developer does not receive your journal or Google account data through normal app use.

2. Data the app accesses and stores

The app stores the drink details you enter: drink type, caffeine amount, serving volume when relevant, selected date and time, time zone, creation timestamp and optional notes. It also stores your settings, unfinished drink draft, history selections and operational information needed to display statistics, notifications, export status and queued work.

If you connect Google Drive, the app uses your selected Google account's email address and Drive account identifier to associate the correct account with your destination. It stores authorized folder and file identifiers, export associations, authorization receipts and transfer status on the phone. These details prevent accidental exports to the wrong destination and let the app retry its work.

Google handles account selection and authorization. The app obtains access tokens through Google's Android authorization services to make authorized API requests. It does not ask for or receive your Google password. Tokens and device authorization are not included in the app's full backups.

The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, Gmail or unrestricted Drive access. Android notification permission is optional and is used for app alerts and transfer results.

3. How optional Google Drive access works

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits work with files the app creates and existing files or folders you explicitly authorize through Google. Selecting a parent folder does not automatically grant access to all existing contents. The app does not request separate email or profile scopes; account identity is obtained through the selected account and Drive's account information.

Within that authorization, the app can:

  • Create and update individual drink JSON exports, including date, caffeine amount, timestamps, optional volume and notes. It may add no-caffeine markers for gaps between recorded dates.
  • Create manual JSON history snapshots for all records or a chosen date range, and replace an existing snapshot when you confirm.
  • Create full app backups containing the journal database, settings, saved account and Drive associations, operational records and unfinished draft; read authorized backup files when you choose recovery.
  • Read file and folder metadata to verify access and organize associated exports into drinks/, exports/ and backups/.
  • Delete a linked individual drink export when you delete its journal entry, and move eligible older app backups to Drive Trash according to your retention setting.

Drive is used for these visible app features only. Changes made to exported drink files in Drive are not imported into your journal. Restoring a full backup is a separate action that replaces local app state after your confirmation; it is not continuous synchronization between devices.

You can choose Wi-Fi or mobile data, Wi-Fi only, or disable Drive transfers in Settings. Android scheduling, connectivity and Google authorization can delay work. An already submitted request may finish after a setting changes.

4. Sharing and use of Google user data

The app sends journal exports, backup contents and the account/file information necessary to perform your requested operations to Google services. Google processes that information under its own privacy policy and applicable service terms. Files remain in your Google Drive account and are subject to the sharing permissions you set there. Anyone you grant access to a destination or file may be able to read it.

The developer does not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models. The app does not send this data to the developer for human review. If you voluntarily provide information in a support request, that information is used to respond to the request; do not send journal data or credentials.

Caffeine Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention, disconnection and deletion

On the phone: journal records remain until you delete them or remove the app's local storage. Deleting a record hides it from History and, if it has a linked Drive export, queues removal of that export. Pending deletion records and associations can remain locally until that work finishes. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app's private local data, including its local recovery copy, and does not delete existing Drive files.

On Google Drive: individual exports and manual snapshots remain until removed by the app's described actions or by you in Drive. Deleting a journal entry queues permanent deletion of its linked individual export when authorized transfers are available. It does not remove that entry from previously created manual snapshots or backups. Manage those copies separately in Drive.

Full backups: the default is one backup per day with five compatible copies retained after Drive is connected. You can change the schedule and copy count or turn automatic backups off. Older eligible backups are moved to Drive Trash only after a new backup has been uploaded and verified. Trash contents follow Google's retention rules. A local safety copy of the state before the last restore is kept for undo and replaced at the next restore; removing app storage also removes it.

Disconnecting: Settings → Disconnect Google Drive pauses the app's saved connections. It preserves your local journal, saved associations and existing Drive files; it is not deletion or Google permission revocation. You can separately revoke access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase files already created. Remove unwanted exports, snapshots, backups and any remaining Trash copies in Drive yourself.

You can inspect, export and delete your own data using the app and Drive. The developer has no central copy of your journal to retrieve or delete. For privacy requests about information you voluntarily shared through support, use the contact route below without publicly posting the information itself. Applicable privacy rights are not limited by this policy.

6. Security and backup sensitivity

Local data is stored in the app's private Android storage. Drive requests use HTTPS and limited file authorization. The app disables Android's standard app backup mechanism and provides its own optional Drive backup feature.

Exports and backups are readable JSON files; a full backup contains an encoded database. Encoding and checksums are not encryption. The app does not add end-to-end encryption to these files. Use a private Drive folder, protect your device and Google account, and avoid sensitive details in journal notes. No storage or network service can guarantee absolute security.

7. This information website and support

This static website does not request Google permissions, accept journal uploads, set application cookies, or use analytics, advertising scripts or third-party fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives normal web request information, including IP addresses, and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Opening external links takes you to services with their own privacy practices. Support issues on GitHub are public, and GitHub processes account and issue data under its own terms. Please do not post personal records, email addresses, account IDs, screenshots containing personal data, backup files, access tokens or credentials.

8. Contact, children and policy updates

For general support or privacy questions, contact LachimB through the support tracker. If your concern requires a private exchange, ask for a private contact route without including the concern's personal details in the public issue.

The app is intended for personal self-tracking and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app.

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. A new use of Google user data will be disclosed and, where required, consent requested before that use begins.
