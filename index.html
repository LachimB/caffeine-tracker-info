A JOURNAL THAT FITS YOUR DAY

Small entries.
Useful perspective.

Caffeine Tracker is a personal Android app, maintained by LachimB. It helps you record caffeine intake and review the history you choose to keep.

01

Record the details

Log a drink with its caffeine amount, date and time, and an optional note. Use one editable drink list for logging and History, with estimated starter recipes. Define caffeine in milligrams or espresso equivalents. Ask for a serving size when logging, choose your own volume or a named size, and give each size its own caffeine amount or espresso count. Recipe changes affect future logs; saved records retain their original details.

02

Start with Today

See your logged caffeine and today’s entries together. Open the separate Add drink screen from the button below the summary, or browse History and statistics. An optional daily target shows progress even with notifications disabled. Preview the selected date’s total before logging or correcting; moving an entry shows both affected dates. Correct a saved drink in place, or undo the latest drink change or day review. Undo defaults to 15 seconds and is configurable from 1 to 300 seconds. Your journal works locally without a Google account.

03

Choose your own backup

Optionally connect Google Drive for one-way drink exports, manual history snapshots and full app backups. Restore a supported backup when you need to recover your journal.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE CONNECTION

Your journal.
Your Drive folder.

Choose a Google account and a destination using Google's account and file selection screens. Caffeine Tracker asks for access to files it creates or you explicitly authorize, using the limited drive.file permission.

The connection lets the app create exports and backups, read authorized backups for recovery, and manage its associated files. It does not request unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

  3. Stay in controlDisconnect in Settings, revoke Google access, or manage your files in Drive.

YOUR DRINKS, YOUR SERVINGS

Define it once.
Log it your way.

Add, edit, reorder or archive recipes in one drink catalogue. Set a caffeine estimate per serving or reference volume, or use espresso equivalents with an editable caffeine amount per espresso. Choose simple volumes or named sizes such as Small, Medium and Large. Each size can calculate caffeine by volume or use a separately entered amount or espresso count.

Existing drinks, saved dose settings and historical entries are preserved when upgrading. Named sizes keep the consumed volume and caffeine total in History. Zero-caffeine recipes can also be recorded; caffeine-focused totals and drink statistics count positive caffeine amounts. Existing linked coffee and tea relationships and their recorded choice summaries remain available.

Optional AI assistance can suggest a caffeine estimate when you leave the amount blank. Add your own OpenAI API key in Settings to enable the wand, then review its suggested amount and serving assumptions before using it. Requests send only the drink name and optional reference or serving volume to OpenAI. Local manual logging works without a key. Read about optional AI and key storage.

CORRECTIONS WITH CONTEXT

Your records.
Your pace.

Undo keeps its original deadline through navigation and restarting. Automatic exports, automatic backups and new daily alerts wait for that window; a request already sent may still finish. Manual snapshots remain historical copies.

Last known caffeine uses only positive entries with a known consumption time that has been reached. Today shows missing-time coverage; zero-caffeine entries stay visible without inflating the count. Unknown consumption times stay unknown unless you enter a time. Future dates and times can be saved after a warning, and are labelled in History. Totals describe logged intake: a future calendar date enters current summaries on that date, while a future time today already counts in today’s logged total. An empty date does not prove a caffeine-free day. Review a date in Today or History to explicitly confirm a complete or caffeine-free record. Positive caffeine prevents caffeine-free confirmation; future dates and entries timed in the future must wait for review. Changing entries reopens affected dates, including both dates when moving a drink. Confirmation adds completeness information without changing caffeine averages.

RECORDED TIMES AND CHOICES

Understand your timing.

Optional timing insights use recorded drink dates and observed exact times. You can set a personal cutoff, show more timing detail on Today, inspect first and last caffeine by date, and browse 7-day, 28-day or calendar-week periods. Cutoff amounts use each drink’s recorded local clock. Earlier/later comparisons use eligible confirmed, fully timed days with compatible time zones; missing times, future consumption, short history and incomplete periods are disclosed. Historical coffee/tea choice views retain explicit logged-choice counts and shares, with matching previous-period boundaries. These are observations of your log, not caffeine-saved estimates or sleep predictions.

FROM YOUR HOME SCREEN

Your recipes, closer at hand.

Home widgets show today’s logged caffeine and caffeinated-drink count. Choose a drink from the same catalogue for each widget, using Widget setup or Settings → Home widgets. Enlarge an existing small widget to show recipe, linked-alternative and setup actions; small widgets retain the read-only summary and tap-to-open-Today route. Logging opens an app review and saves only when you confirm. Prompted serving sizes are resolved first, including sizes with their own caffeine dose and no volume. Explicit alternatives create only the consumed drink. Use the shared Undo window or open Today to correct a saved record.

GOOD TO KNOW

A journal, with clear boundaries.

Caffeine amounts and statistics are estimates for personal awareness. The app does not provide medical advice or determine a safe amount for you. Drive exports are one-way; they are not live synchronization between devices.

This website provides information about the app. It does not collect your journal, connect to your Google account, or offer an APK download.
