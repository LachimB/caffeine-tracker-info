A JOURNAL THAT FITS YOUR DAY

Small entries.
Useful perspective.

Caffeine Tracker is a personal Android app, maintained by LachimB. It helps you record caffeine intake and review the history you choose to keep.

01

Record the details

Log a drink with its caffeine amount, date and time, and an optional note. Use drink presets or enter an amount that matches your serving. Saved entries keep the amount recorded at the time.

02

Look back, simply

Browse your history and intake statistics to understand your own habits. Your journal works locally without connecting a Google account.

03

Choose your own backup

Optionally connect Google Drive for one-way drink exports, manual history snapshots and full app backups. Restore a supported backup when you need to recover your journal.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE CONNECTION

Your journal.
Your Drive folder.

Choose a Google account and a destination using Google's account and file selection screens. Caffeine Tracker asks for access to files it creates or you explicitly authorize, using the limited drive.file permission.

The connection lets the app create exports and backups, read authorized backups for recovery, and manage its associated files. It does not request unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

Understand the data and permissions →
  1. Choose what to connectGoogle Drive is optional. Local journaling stays available without it.
  2. Set the paceChoose Wi-Fi or mobile data, Wi-Fi only, or disable Drive transfers.
  3. Stay in controlDisconnect in Settings, revoke Google access, or manage your files in Drive.

GOOD TO KNOW

A journal, with clear boundaries.

Caffeine amounts and statistics are estimates for personal awareness. The app does not provide medical advice or determine a safe amount for you. Drive exports are one-way; they are not live synchronization between devices.

This website provides information about the app. It does not collect your journal, connect to your Google account, or offer an APK download.

Questions or feedback?

Use the public support tracker for general app questions. Never include journal entries, account details, backups or credentials in a public issue.
