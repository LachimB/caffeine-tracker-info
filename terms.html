2. Estimates and personal responsibility

Drink caffeine content varies by product, preparation, serving size and other factors. Presets, totals, statistics and alerts rely on your entries and configured values. They can be incomplete or inaccurate.

For personal awareness

The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a personalized safe caffeine limit. Do not rely on it to make medical decisions. Consult a qualified health professional about caffeine and your individual circumstances.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the entries and settings you choose. Check a product label or another reliable source when entering caffeine amounts.

Google services are governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for your Google account, storage quota, file-sharing settings and network charges. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Check the app's transfer and backup status rather than assuming an operation finished.

4. Your records, backups and deletion

You retain your rights in the data you enter. Keep any independent copies you need. Full backups contain personal journal data and app settings; use a private destination. The app does not add end-to-end encryption to exports or backup files.

Restoring a backup replaces the local app state after confirmation. A supported restore preserves a local undo copy, but that copy cannot survive removal of app storage. Review the selected backup before proceeding.

Deleting a journal entry can permanently delete its linked individual Drive export once authorized transfers are available. Previously created history snapshots and full backups are separate copies and may still contain that entry. Disconnecting Drive does not erase local or remote data. Backup retention may move older eligible copies to Drive Trash after a new backup is verified. See retention and deletion for details.

Do not use the app or its support channels to share data you are not authorized to use, expose other people's personal information, or transmit credentials. Never attach journal databases or backups to public support issues.
