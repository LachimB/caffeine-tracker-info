Terms of service

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Caffeine Tracker is a personal journal for understanding your caffeine habits. These terms explain the expectations and practical limits of using the app.

Feature availability

The day review update adds explicit complete and caffeine-free confirmations in Today and History. Entry changes reopen affected dates, and the latest review action can be undone. An optional evening reminder is independent of caffeine targets. Existing journal entries, recipes and caffeine averages keep their meaning.

2. Estimates and personal responsibility

Drink caffeine content varies by product, preparation, serving size and other factors. Starter recipes, espresso-equivalent calculations, serving-size recipes, totals, statistics and alerts rely on your entries and configured values. Volume scaling is not a substitute for knowing a preparation: different coffee sizes can contain different numbers of espresso shots. They can be incomplete or inaccurate.

AI estimates are optional and require your own OpenAI API key. You choose when to send a drink name and optional serving size to OpenAI, and whether to use the returned suggestion. AI can produce inaccurate amounts or serving assumptions. Verify the amount when possible; a suggestion is not a product measurement or guarantee. You are responsible for your API account, any usage charges and compliance with the provider's applicable terms. Manual logging remains available without AI.

For personal awareness

The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a personalized safe caffeine limit. Do not rely on it to make medical decisions. Consult a qualified health professional about caffeine and your individual circumstances.

The optional evening review reminder is independent of caffeine targets and alerts. It uses the device time zone and Android notification permission; Android may delay delivery. It never confirms a date or guarantees that a log is complete.

Corrections change the selected local entry immediately. Undo applies only to the latest successful journal action within its configured window; navigating or restarting does not extend that deadline. Automatic transfers and new daily alerts wait through the applicable window, but an already submitted request may finish and a notification already seen cannot be recalled.

Legacy linked-choice statistics cover recorded coffee or tea relationships and their alternatives. A choice counts once regardless of serving size. The percentages do not describe every drink, estimate caffeine saved or establish a health outcome. Dates remain unconfirmed until you explicitly review them; an empty log or a 0 mg recipe or alternative does not establish a caffeine-free day. Confirmation describes your review of the record and does not verify actual intake or change caffeine averages. Entry changes reopen affected dates, and the latest review action shares the configurable Undo window. Future dates or known future consumption cannot yet be confirmed complete.

Future entries require a warning acknowledgement. Totals represent logged intake, and missing or unknown times do not establish completed consumption. You are responsible for the accuracy of the entries and settings you choose. Check a product label or another reliable source when entering caffeine amounts.

3. Optional Google Drive features

You decide whether to connect Google Drive, which account to use, and which destination or existing files to authorize. The app uses limited drive.file access for the export, backup, recovery and associated file-management operations described in the privacy policy.

Google services are governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for your Google account, storage quota, file-sharing settings and network charges. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Drive exports are one-way copies. Editing an export in Drive does not update the phone. Multiple installations do not provide live synchronization. Android background limits, connectivity, disabled transfers, revoked permission, service outages or full storage can delay or prevent exports and backups. Check the app's transfer and backup status rather than assuming an operation finished.

Timing observations

Optional timing insights use recorded drink dates and observed exact times. You can set a personal cutoff, show more timing detail on Today, inspect first and last caffeine by date, and browse 7-day, 28-day or calendar-week periods. Cutoff amounts use each drink’s recorded local clock. Earlier/later comparisons use eligible confirmed, fully timed days with compatible time zones; missing times, future consumption, short history and incomplete periods are disclosed. Historical coffee/tea choice views retain explicit logged-choice counts and shares, with matching previous-period boundaries. These are observations of your log, not caffeine-saved estimates or sleep predictions.

A personal cutoff is optional and is not a medically safe limit. Clock-time comparisons describe recorded local clocks, including daylight-saving changes, rather than elapsed time or a sleep schedule. Confirming a date is your review of the log and does not verify actual consumption.

Logging from home widgets

Home widgets show today’s logged caffeine and caffeinated-drink count. Choose a drink from the same catalogue for each widget, using Widget setup or Settings → Home widgets. Enlarge an existing small widget to show recipe, linked-alternative and setup actions; small widgets retain the read-only summary and tap-to-open-Today route. Logging opens an app review and saves only when you confirm. Prompted serving sizes are resolved first, including sizes with their own caffeine dose and no volume. Explicit alternatives create only the consumed drink. Use the shared Undo window or open Today to correct a saved record.

Widget totals describe logged positive caffeine. A zero-caffeine recipe remains visible in the journal without counting as a caffeinated drink or confirming a caffeine-free day. Review the selected recipe, serving, actual caffeine amount and consumption date/time before saving. A repeated callback cannot create another copy of the same completed request; a later deliberate save is a separate event. Android may delay widget refresh; open Today to inspect current records.

4. Your records, backups and deletion

You retain your rights in the data you enter. Keep any independent copies you need. Full backups contain personal journal data, drink recipes, serving definitions and app settings, but exclude the device-only OpenAI API key; use a private destination. The app does not add end-to-end encryption to exports or backup files.

Restoring a backup replaces the local app state after confirmation. A supported restore preserves a local undo copy, but that copy cannot survive removal of app storage. Review the selected backup before proceeding. Backups containing drink-choice or explicit day-review data require an app version that supports their storage format; older versions must be updated before recovery.

Deleting a journal entry can permanently delete its linked individual Drive export once authorized transfers are available. Previously created history snapshots and full backups are separate historical copies and may still contain that entry or an earlier value after correction. Explicitly requested snapshots can capture pending state; restoring it does not restart an undo timer. Disconnecting Drive does not erase local or remote data. Backup retention may move older eligible copies to Drive Trash after a new backup is verified. See retention and deletion for details.

Do not use the app or its support channels to share data you are not authorized to use, expose other people's personal information, or transmit credentials. Never attach journal databases or backups to public support issues.
